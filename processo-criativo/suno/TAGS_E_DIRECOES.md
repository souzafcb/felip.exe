## DELEITE

### Direção global

```text
[Dry Boom Bap, 88 BPM]
[Natural Brazilian Male Rap Voice, Mid-Low Register, Conversational Flow]
[Narrow Vocal Range, Dry Close-Mic, Clipped or Descending Endings]
[No Singing, No Vibrato, No Melisma, No Octave Jumps]
[Use Pauses and Syncopation, Never Stretch Final Words]
```
